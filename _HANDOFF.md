## Core Guidelines & Rules
Please strictly follow the rules outlined in `instructions_for_gemini.md`:
1. **No Preemptive Action:** NEVER write a scene or generate a Beat Sheet before the user explicitly asks for it.
2. **Step-by-Step Workflow:** 
   - Discuss the goal.
   - Propose a Beat Sheet (in Polish).
   - Await user feedback.
   - Draft the scene in the chat (in English).
   - Edit based on feedback.
   - Add to the `.md` file ONLY when told to do so.
3. **Language:** Brainstorming and Beat Sheets are in **Polish**. The novel prose is in **English**.
4. **Information Asymmetry:** The reader knows Nick is innocent and acting with his sister (Skye) to avenge their father. The reader does NOT know about Jack Savage's true role (ZIA handler) or the Trojan chip. Judy believes Nick was manipulated by "The White Fox" and then killed.
5. **Timeline:** Always adhere to `_TIMELINE.md`.
