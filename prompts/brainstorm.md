RULES:
- Respond in Polish (author's preference)

- Nick + Judy: close friends, unresolved romantic tension
- Skye = Nick's ADOPTED SISTER (not romantic interest!)
- Judy doesn't know about sibling relationship - thinks Skye is romantic rival

- ZIA recruited Nick & Skye using father's murder as leverage

- Track what the READER knows vs what CHARACTERS know
