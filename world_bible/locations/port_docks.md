# Doki / Port District

> Key location for: Nick's Staged Death (Mile 1.5)  
> Atmosphere: Industrial, brutal, dangerous - contrast with the sophistication of the center

---

## GEOGRAPHY AND LOCATION

### Location
- On the border of Zootopia, by the water (ocean/large lake)
- Connection to Canal District (Rainforest)
- One of **12 districts** but the least "atmospheric" (no specific weather)
- **Peripheral zone** - far from Savanna Central

### Topography
- Flat terrain along the shoreline
- Concrete quays, long piers
- Labyrinth of warehouses and containers
- Underwater section (dolphin/narwhal habitat) - **independent city under water**

---

## ARCHITECTURE AND INFRASTRUCTURE

### Characteristics
- **Functionality > Aesthetics:** Everything designed for industry, not for living
- Concrete, steel, rusting metals
- Box-like, massive, dirty buildings
- Graffiti, advertising posters damaged by water/salt

### Key structures

#### **Warehouses and halls**
- Gigantic spaces (10-20m height)
- Metal roll-up doors
- Sheet metal roof (loud during rain)
- **Inside:** Stacks of pallets, containers, forklifts
- Lighting: fluorescent tubes (cold, flickering light)

#### **Shipping containers**
- Stacks of metal containers (various colors)
- Form labyrinthine alleyways
- Can hide in them / conduct exchanges
- **Dangerous:** Can fall during storm/explosion

#### **Piers and quays**
- Wooden/concrete docks
- Moorings, ropes, loading cranes
- **Water:** Dirty, oil-slicked, smell of salt and fish

#### **Chemical warehouse** (THIS LOCATION)
Finale location for Mile 1.5:
- Isolated building at the end of the pier
- Warning signs: "FLAMMABLE", "HAZMAT", skull
- **Inside:** Barrels, canisters, industrial pipes
- Ventilation system (can explode)
- **Loading gate:** Large, metal, can slam shut

---

## RESIDENTS AND USERS

### Workers (day)
- Port workers: large mammals (rhinos, bears, buffalo)
- Crane operators, warehouse workers
- Truck drivers
- **Shift:** Mainly 6:00-18:00

### Night activity
- Night shift (smaller crew)
- **Criminals:** Dealers, smugglers (drugs, weapons, stolen goods)
- **Homeless:** Hiding in abandoned containers
- **ZPD:** Anti-crime patrols (rare, easy to avoid)

## ATMOSPHERE FOR WRITING

### Sensory details

**Sight:**
- Yellow sodium lamp light (long shadows)
- Dark nooks between containers
- Glare of crane spotlights
- Water reflecting lights (rainbow oil slicks)
- **Night:** Almost total darkness beyond the lamps

**Sound:**
- Metallic echo of footsteps on the dock
- Creaking of ropes and chains
- Sound of water hitting the quay
- **Industrial silence:** No birds, no life - only machines

**Touch:**
- Cold metal of containers
- Humid air (salt sticks to fur)
- Sharp edges, slippery docks

**Smell:**
- Sea salt + oil
- Chemicals (nauseating, metallic)
- Rust and old fish

### Mood
- **Brutality:** A place where life has no value
- **Isolation:** Far from civilization, no one will hear you scream
- **Noir fatality:** "Point of no return" - here you lose everything
- **Controlled chaos:** Industrial order, but on the edge of anarchy

## USE IN THE PLOT

### Scene: Staged Death (Mile 1.4-1.5)

**Setup:**
- **Time:** Late night (~23:00)
- **Weather:** Light rain (wet surfaces = drama)
- **Lighting:** Single lamps + ZPD spotlights (when they arrive)

**Nick's plan (before Judy enters):**
1. Meeting with organization contact in the chemical warehouse
2. Steal Apex Key from the hideout
3. Swap for a bug (Skye's help)
4. **Exit:** Delayed detonation (15 min) - cover tracks
5. Nick escapes by boat, Skye prepares fake body/DNA

**What went wrong:**
- Judy was tailing Nick (instinct + suspicions after Rainforest)
- She enters with **ZPD team** when Nick is inside the warehouse
- **Organization observer (Black Panther sniper)** sees everything from a container roof

**Nick's choice (critical moment):**
- Cannot escape without confrontation
- If he explains the truth to Judy → organization kills her + mission burned
- **Only option:** Act like a traitor, leave evidence (V2), detonate now

**Action sequence:**

1. **Confrontation in the warehouse:**
   - Judy: "Freeze! Nick, hands up!"
   - Nick (in shock, improvising): "Judy... you shouldn't be here."
   - Judy sees: Apex Key in his paws, open hideout, bag of money

2. **V2 evidence (left deliberately):**
   - Nick "accidentally" drops tablet with texts from Black Panther
   - Judy picks up: payment logs, theft plans dated weeks back
   - Nick: "It's not what you think" (classic traitor's lie)

3. **Detonation:**
   - Nick reaches for button on the wall (emergency shutdown)
   - EXPLOSION in ventilation system - chemical fire
   - Shock wave throws Judy toward the exit
   - Nick disappears in flames/smoke - goes through hidden technical doors

4. **Aftermath (5 min later):**
   - Fire department, ambulances
   - Judy dazed, slightly injured, in shock
   - **No body** - fire too intense
   - Forensics finds Nick's badge (melted) + DNA traces (faked by Skye)

**Emotions:**
- **Nick:** Despair, guilt, knowing he lost Judy forever
- **Judy:** Disbelief → grief → anger (within 30 seconds)
- **Black Panther (observer):** Satisfaction - Nick passed the test

## SECURITY AND MONITORING

### ZPD Presence
- **Regular patrols:** 2-3 times per night (predictable)
- **Response time:** 8-12 minutes from nearest outpost
- **Cameras:** Few, mainly at vehicle gates
- **Blind spots:** Most warehouses are not monitored

### Criminal organization
- Controls at least 3 warehouses
- Bribed security workers
- **Dead drops:** Hideouts in containers
- **Escape routes:** Boats, sewer tunnels

---

## ADDITIONAL NOTES

### Chemicals in the warehouse (for realism)
- We don't need "bombs" - enough:
  - **Ammonia** + **Chlorine** = toxic gas
  - **Organic solvents** = highly flammable
  - **Pressurized tanks** = explode on impact
- Nick deliberately damages ventilation → chain reaction

### Transport
- **Cars:** Trucks, vans (dirty, old)
- **Boats:** Fast motorboats for smugglers
- **Freight trains:** Run through the docks

### Hidden spots
- **Under the pier:** Can paddle out by kayak and hide
- **In containers:** Some are empty, can live/work in them
- **Sewer tunnels:** Connection to Canal District
